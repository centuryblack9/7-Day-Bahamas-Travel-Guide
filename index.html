Day 3 Framework Great Exuma • Elizabeth Harbour Hub
Transit: 45m Domestic Hopper

Nassau to Great Exuma

45-minute domestic morning flight to George Town. Cross to Stocking Island via quick water taxi across Elizabeth Harbour for empty beaches and beachside lunch. Base around George Town.

Morning 08:30
Fly to George Town (GGT)

Board Bahamasair or Western Air for the scenic low-altitude crossing over shimmering sapphire sandbars.

Midday 11:30
Stocking Island Water Taxi

Catch the 10-minute water shuttle from Government Dock across Elizabeth Harbour to Chat 'N' Chill beach.

Afternoon & Evening
Stingray Beach & Town Base

Wade with friendly wild stingrays in shallows before returning to George Town for sunset dinner.

Day 4 Framework The Exuma Cays • Staniel Cay Arc
Pacing: All-Day Powerboat Expedition

The Exuma Cays

Full-day excursion north to Staniel Cay area. See swimming pigs at Big Major Cay, explore Thunderball Grotto at low tide, and swim with nurse sharks at Compass Cay. Listen to guides carefully.

Stop 1 • Big Major
Swimming Pigs

Feed fruit from the water as the famous island pigs swim out to meet arriving boats in turquoise shallows.

Stop 2 • Slack Low Tide
Thunderball Grotto

Snorkel beneath submerged rock archways into the illuminated James Bond 007 cavern filled with yellowtail snapper.

Stop 3 • Compass Cay
Nurse Shark Marina

Submerge on the low-tide wooden dock beside dozens of docile nurse sharks under certified marina supervision.

Crucial Marine Briefing: Never touch nurse shark dorsal fins or feed them from closed fists. Thunderball Grotto can only be entered safely during slack low tide due to swift tidal currents.

Day 5 Framework Eleuthera • Coastal Discovery
Vehicle: Left-Hand Driving

Great Exuma to Eleuthera

Mid-day flight connection back through Nassau hub to Governor's Harbour. Pick up a local rental vehicle (drive on left). Explore Banks Road area or relax at a quiet beach settlement.

Morning 10:30
GGT → NAS → GHB

Hop the short inter-island connector into Governor's Harbour Airport (GHB) in central Eleuthera.

Afternoon 13:30
Vehicle Pickup & Banks Rd

Collect an SUV with sufficient clearance. Drive south along Banks Road, stopping at French Leave Beach.

Evening 18:30
Governor's Settlement

Wander past 19th-century Victorian clapboard homes and dine on stone crab claws overlooking Cupid's Cay.

Navigational Advisory: Queen's Highway

Eleuthera is 110 miles long and only 1 mile wide. There are few streetlights; plan daytime driving and remember that all vehicles drive on the left side of the road.

Day 6 Framework Harbour Island • Dunmore Town
Transit: 5-Min Water Shuttle

Harbour Island

Drive north to Three Island Dock, catch a 5-minute water taxi, and rent a golf cart. Explore Dunmore Town pastel streets and lounge at world-renowned Pink Sands Beach.

Morning 09:00
Three Island Dock & Taxi

Park near Three Island Dock. Board the continuous $5/person water shuttle directly to Dunmore Town Government Dock.

Midday 11:30
Pink Sands Beach

Wander 3 miles of powdered coral shoreline tinted soft blush pink by crushed foraminifera shells.

Afternoon 15:00
Dunmore Cart Cruising

Glide past pastel picket fences, bougainvillea blossoms, and savor conch fritters at Queen Conch.

Island Transportation Norm

Cars are rare on Harbour Island. Golf carts are the definitive mode of transport and can be arranged immediately as you step off the Government Dock.

Day 7 Framework North Eleuthera • Departure Operations
Protocol: Heavy Buffer Window

Return Connections & Departure

Catch the earliest water taxi back to main Eleuthera island, route to North Eleuthera Airport (ELH), and fly back to Nassau with a heavy buffer window before your international departure check-in.

Morning 07:45
Early Water Taxi Run

Midmorning 09:30
North Eleuthera (ELH)

Taxi 15 minutes to ELH. Board the short domestic connection to Lynden Pindling (NAS) in Nassau.

Afternoon 14:00+
US CBP Pre-Clearance at NAS

Valid passport with 6 months validity required. US citizens complete homeland customs inside Nassau (NAS) before departure.

Reef Protection
